Starvation and Aging 

Definition : is a condition in an operating system where a process waits 
indefinitely for cpu time or other resources because other processes keep getting priority. 

ex : suppose the cpu scheduler uses priority scheduling . 

process p1 has priority 1 
process p2 has priority 2 
process p3 has priority 3 
process p4 has priority 4 

assume a smaller number means higher priority . if high -priority processes keep arriving , p4 may never get cpu time because the scheudler always chooses higher - priority processess first . 

this is called starvation 

how to prevent startvation we use a technique called aging. 


Aging 

Definition : is a technique used by the operating system to prevent starvation by gradually increasing the priority of a process the longer it waits in the ready queue. 


Example:

Suppose P3 has priority 10, where a smaller number means higher priority.

Waiting time	       Priority of P3
Initially	           10
After 5 seconds	           8
After 10 seconds	   6
After 15 seconds	   4
 
as p3 waits longer , its priority imporves. eventually it gets cpu time even if higher-priority processess keep arrving. 

this prevents a process from waiting indefinitely
