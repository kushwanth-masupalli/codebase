what is Race condition ?

a race condition occurs when multiple threads or process access shared data concurrently , and final result depends on the order in which they execute.

how do we prevent race conditions ?
 by synchronizing the access to shared resource using mechanisms such as mutextes, semaphores ,monitors , atomic operatoins or concurrency primitives .

What is Critical Section problem ?

a critical section is the part of a program where a process or thread accesses shared resources .the critical section problem is about designing a mechanism that allows safe access to that shared resource when multiple processes execute concurrently.

--- A correct solution must satisfy three requirements : 

1 ) Mutual Exclusion  = at most one process can execute its critcal section at at time all other process will wait

2) Progress  = if no process is currently inside the critical section and some process want to enter the system , the sytem should not unnecessarliy prevent one of them from entering.

3) Bounded Waiting : a process should not wait indefinitely to enter its critical section after requesting access . 
this prevents starvation .

B)))What is Mutex ?

a mutex or mutual exlusion lock , is a synchronization mechanism that ensures only one thread can enter a critical section at a time.

typical flow is like lock() -> run the critical section -> unlock()


what is Busy waiting ?
in busy waiting , a thread continously checks whether the lock is available.

what is the difference between Busy waiting and blocking ?
busy waiting continously consumes CPU while waiting, whereas blocking puts the waiting thread to sleep so the CPU can execute other work. Blocking is generally preferable when the expected wait is long.
