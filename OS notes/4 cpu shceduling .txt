CPU Scheducling : is the mechanism used by the operating system to decide which ready process should get the cpu next . 


the main goals are : 

maximize cpu utilization , maximize throughput, minimize waiting time, minimize turnaround time , minimize response time 

why do we need scheduling ? 
 
if there is only one process , scheduling isn't much of a problem , but when multiple process compute for the cpu , the os needs a policy to decide who runs and for how long.

FCFS = first come first served 

SJF = shortest job first  = sjf selects the process with the smallest CPU burst time to exectue next .

ROUND ROBIN  = is a preemptive scheduling algorithm where each process gets a fixed amount of 
CPU time called the time quantum . after it quantum expires, the process is preempted and 
moved to the back of the ready queue.
this algo provide fair CPU sharing . 

non-preemptive = once a process gets the CPU ,the OS does not forcibly take the CPU away. the process gives it up when it finishes ,blocks or voluntarily yields.

preemptive  =the OS can forcibly take the CPU away from a currently running process and give it to another process.

PRIORITY SCHEDULING : in priority scheduling , each process is assigned a priority , and the process with the highest priority is selected to run .
