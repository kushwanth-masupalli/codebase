1) What is computer network ?

a computer network is a collectoin of interconnected devices such as  computers, servers, routers, and switches that communicate with each other and share data or resources 

why do we need networks

communication = computers can exchange data.
resource sharing = printers, storage ,internet connections etc
centralized services = servers can provide services to many clients 
distributed computing = different machines can work together 
scalability = applications can communicate across multiple machines.


2) Type of networks 

i) PAN  = personal Area Network 

is a very small network around an individual , typically within a few meters 
ex: phone  -> bluetooth -> earbuds 

ii) LAN = local Area network 

lan connects devices within a relatively small geographical area such as a home , office,school,or building .

typical technologies = ethernet 

LAN generally provides high speed and low latency because the devices are geogrphically close.

iii) MAN = metropolitan Area Network 

man connects multiple lans acorss city or metropolitan area

it is larger than a  lan but smaller than a wan.

iv) WAN = Wide Area Network 

wan connects networks accross large geographical ares such as countires or continetnts.
the internet is the largest example of a WAN .

4) Bandwith  = is the maximum amount of data that a  network connection can theoretically transmit per unit of time .
 Throughput = is the actual amount of data successfully transferred per unit of time.

 latency =  is the amount of time taken for data to travel from the source to the destination.
