an er Model has three main components :

2) attribute 
3) realtionship 

---Entity :

ex student, employee

---Attribute : an attribute describe an entity like what actually there in the entity 
for exam a student has rollno and name etc... rollno and name all these are attributes

1) simple attribute , compostie attribute , single valued attribute,mulit valued attribute,

---Relationship 

a relationship describes how entites are connected 

relationship is shown using daimond


Degree of Relationship : 
1) Unary = same entity relates to itself 
2) Binary = two entites in one relatoinship
3) Ternary = three entites in one relationship


Cardinality 

tells us how many entites can participate in a realtionship 

1) one to one
2) one to many 
3) many to one
4) many to many


degree tell how many entities can participate and cardinality tells how many instances or records or rows can be associtead with instances of another entity
