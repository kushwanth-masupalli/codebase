1. Log-Based Recovery

Before modifying actual database data, the DBMS records information about the operation in a log stored on durable storage.

2. UNDO vs REDO


UNDO = Reverse changes made by transactions that did not successfully commit.

REDO = Reapply changes from a transaction that committed, but whose updated database pages may not have reached disk before the crash.

3. Checkpointing

Imagine the database has been running for 10 hours and has generated a huge log:

If the database crashes, recovery might have to examine a huge amount of history.

A checkpoint periodically records a recovery point/state.

Conceptually:After a crash, the recovery process can often avoid processing irrelevant history before the checkpoint.

4) Shadow paging = 
Shadow paging is an alternative recovery technique that doesn't rely on traditional undo/redo logging.
